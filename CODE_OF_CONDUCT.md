# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be someone others can work with. Assume good faith, argue about the code rather
than the person, and accept that you will sometimes be wrong in public.

## Expected behaviour

- Give feedback on the work: what is wrong, why it matters, what would fix it.
- Accept feedback the same way. A review comment is about a diff, not about you.
- Respect that contributors differ in experience, language, timezone and context.
- When you disagree with a decision, say so once, clearly, with your reasoning.
  If the maintainers decide otherwise, that is the end of it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, demeaning comments, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Harassment of any kind, including unwanted contact after being asked to stop.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and any other
space where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainers through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/itsshreyasbhardwaj-design/dataflow-studio/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting them directly. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this document, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge harmful.
Enforcement decisions are explained to the person concerned.

This document adapts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
